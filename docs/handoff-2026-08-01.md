# Handoff — 2026-08-01, the viewport/density session and the fine-tuning after it

The session started as "debug a CI failure", became a mobile-layout pass, and then
a fine-tuning pass. What follows is the state, the decisions already made (so they
are not relitigated), what is open, and the traps that cost time.

**Second half, after the compaction** (see [TODO.md](../TODO.md) for each):

- **A40 — a void declaration leaked to every client.** `redactEventsFor` stripped
  `drew`/`kongReplacement` and nothing else, but the void phase emits
  `voidDeclared { seat, suit }` for all four seats at once, so every client got the
  three suits its own view withholds. `suit` is now `Suit | null`, nulled for
  everyone but the declarer. **The lesson worth keeping: `views.ts` redacts two
  channels, the view and the event log, and this is the second leak to arrive via
  the log.**
- **O2 — bot pacing, both halves.** 700ms per move (was 150), `--bot-delay` to
  retune, `SM_BOT_DELAY_MS=150` pinned in the vitest and Playwright configs. Plus a
  move-history panel, opened from a 🗒 button in the play well — *not* the top bar,
  which has no width left: a fourth icon truncated the turn indicator to "Y...".
- **O4 — one tile face everywhere.** The actual complaint was "the middle discard
  looks more glossy", not the discard pile: the well's last discard was the last
  board tile still on the 3D art. A new `solo` prop gives a lone flat tile the lift
  `.tile-run` would otherwise provide. This also revived the last-discard marker,
  dead on every flat tile since R7 because its CSS matched `.tile-face` only.
- **Discards fly from hand to tray**, as a fixed overlay rather than a transform on
  the tray tile — `viewport.spec.ts` asserts no tile escapes its tray and samples
  every ~130ms, so an animating tray tile would fail it.
- **Both melds gaps R7 left are closed** — your melds row scrolls, and side
  opponents show a compact `MeldChip` (one tile plus the meld's name, because three
  flush tiles are ~96px in an 80px column).

## Decisions already made — do not reopen without a reason

- **Per-seat discard trays stay.** A central pool is a *fallback*, wanted because
  the middle of the board is mostly empty, not a replacement that was agreed.
- **換三張 is off by default** and host-opt-in. It is not in Novikov's ruleset.
- **The bot pace is a pace, not a rule.** It lives in `room.ts`, not `GameConfig`:
  a replay of the same seed is identical at any value, and putting it in the config
  would make it part of the game state and the snapshot.
- **Every tile on the board is flat.** The old "a singleton should look like a
  singleton" rule is gone. The one exception is the overlapped hand-count stack,
  which keeps 3D backs because flat backs overlapped merge into one green slab.
- **Freed horizontal space went to the hand, not to bigger tray tiles.** The hand
  was 19.1px wide against a ~24px readability floor and it is the only thing a
  player taps.
- **The flat cell reproduces the art's own layer order** — outline, `#005f00`
  side, `#cddacd` plate, white, face — on the top and right, plus its top-right
  specular. The measurements are tabulated in TODO. Compressing rather than copying
  is the point: the literal insets would hand back the glyph width R7 won, while
  bands sized to the glyphs' existing margins fit for free — so the glyphs actually
  grew, 89% → 95% of the cell.
- **Bevel on top+right specifically, never left+right.** A right shoulder meets
  the next tile's bare face, so flush neighbours show one shared edge; left+right
  is what doubled up and started the whole flat-tile exercise.
- **Every tile gets the same bevel.** Showing the full side only where nothing
  abutted the tile is more literally correct and was tried, but one tile then looked
  like two depending on where it sat, and a wrapping tray cannot express "last in a
  row" to opt in. Uniform wins.
- **Bands are sized by the glyphs, not by the art.** 9.5% of the height and 14% of
  the width, against the art's 20.6%/22.5%: the glyphs' own margins inside their
  frames are 8.4% top and 16% a side, so that is exactly where a wider band starts
  eating ink. The art's literal proportions were built and rejected — they need the
  glyph inset, which undoes R7 at the 23px hand size.
- **The corner radius is proportional, not a rem** — `18.1% / 14.9%`, measured off
  the art's outline cubics (~38 units of 210×255). A radius that reads as a tile at
  64px is a blob at 23px. `.tile-mark` shares it, so the void screen's marker ring
  can't square off a round tile.
- ~~**Tiles are flush with a bottom bevel.**~~ Superseded: the deep green front
  edge was R7's own invention, and the art has no green on the bottom at all (its
  side layer insets 12.9% from the bottom). The bottom is a thin plate-and-outline
  edge now, with the green on the top and right where the art puts it. Original
  reasoning kept below for the record — fully flat *did* read as a printed sheet;
  the left/right/top bevels were the ones that doubled up between neighbours.
- ~~**Singletons keep the 3D art**~~ — reversed later the same day; see the tile-face
  entry above.
- **Each flat cell paints its own face**, rather than sharing one surface across a
  run: a lifted hand tile over a shared surface reads as a hole in the strip.

## Open — needs a decision, nothing started

Also recorded as O1–O4 in
[ARCHITECTURE.md §12](../ARCHITECTURE.md#12-open-questions--explicit-deferrals).

**O1. The release binary embeds the tile SVGs, which ARCHITECTURE §13 forbids.**
`scripts/release/gen-embedded-client.mjs` walks `packages/client/dist` and
base64-embeds every file into `embedded-client.ts`, compiled into the Bun binary —
57 SVGs. §13 says the CC-BY-SA boundary depends on those staying "standalone
fetched assets" and not being merged "into compiled JavaScript output". Both cannot
be true. Predates the flat set (the embed is A20) but that set doubled the count.
A licensing call, not a patch: exclude `tiles/` from the embed and ship them beside
the binary, or accept the merge and state the binary's licence. **npm package and
from-source are unaffected** — both serve `tiles/` from disk.

**O2. Bot pacing** — ✅ done, both halves. See the top of this file.

**O3. Central discard pool.** Show every discard in the middle, mark the last one,
and show each player's chosen void suit. The redaction decision it needs:
`PublicPlayer` has no `voidedSuit` — only `you` gets it, and the first discard is
face down precisely so the suit is not leaked early (A37). Reveal it only once that
player flips their first discard, which is when a real table learns it. Note the PDF
edge case: a player may declare a suit they hold none of, in which case a
card-indicator stands in and no tile ever reveals it. **A40 raised the bar:** the
suit used to arrive on every client through the event log regardless, and now
correctly doesn't, so the pool needs that deliberate reveal built rather than a
field that happened to be on the wire. The middle is also no longer quite empty —
the history control sits in its bottom-right corner.

**O4. Discard tile styling** — ✅ done, and it was not about the discard pile. The
specific complaint turned out to be *"the middle discard looks more glossy compared
to the discard and hand tiles"*. My earlier reading — that a flush run reads as
tiles held together and wants standalone 3D tiles for discards — was wrong, and
acting on it would have undone R7. Worth remembering as a case for asking what
specifically looks wrong rather than building the plausible interpretation.

The two lower-priority melds items carried forward here are also done — see the top
of this file.

## Traps that cost time this session

- **Restart the server after any client rebuild.** `@fastify/static` is registered
  with `wildcard: false`, so the asset list is snapshotted at boot. A rebuilt bundle
  404s into the SPA fallback and the page dies with a MIME-type error on a
  `text/html` module script.
- **`pnpm e2e` needs port 8080 free.** Playwright starts its own server with
  `reuseExistingServer: false`; a locally-running server makes the whole suite fail
  before the first test.
- **`VITE_E2E=1` builds are for tests only.** They attach `window.__e2e`; specs fail
  without them. Rebuild *without* it before handing the app to a human.
- **The specs' game is random and unseeded.** `room.ts` uses `randomUUID()`, so a
  local pass proves little — R6 passed locally every time while failing CI three
  times running. Prove layout claims with an injected-spacer probe or a forced
  state, not a lucky round.
- **Playwright's `devices['iPhone SE']` is 320×568, not 375×568.** The whole R6 bug
  was the audit's numbers being measured at 375 while CI asserted at 320.
- **A wrapping flex container defaults to `align-content: stretch`.** Spare
  cross-axis space is handed to the lines and children are drawn *past their aspect
  ratio*. This is what made the side trays' tiles "way too long". `content-start
  items-start` plus `min-h-0` and no `flex-1`.
- **`svg.getBBox()` must be called on the root.** Per-element boxes are in that
  element's own space, and the pin dots sit inside nested transformed groups — one
  measurement pass put pin-1's box 1300 units from every other tile's.
- **The screenshot script could ship the wrong screen.** It used to shoot after its
  play loop, so a bot winning inside 14 moves produced a round-end image in
  `screenshot.png`. Fixed, and it now fails rather than shipping the wrong screen —
  but it is still an unseeded random game, so *look at the output*. Doing exactly
  that is what caught the truncated turn indicator: `git show HEAD:docs/screenshot.png`
  into a scratch file and comparing the two images is a cheap regression check for
  anything on the play screen.
- **The play screen's hand looks clipped in the screenshots. It isn't.** Measured on
  three devices, `.board-felt` has `scrollHeight === clientHeight` and the last hand
  tile's bottom sits 12–15px above the viewport bottom. The captured image is a
  little shorter than `window.innerHeight`, which cuts the bevel off the bottom row
  — present in the pre-R7 screenshot too. Measure before chasing it.
- **The tray guard constrains how discards may animate.** `viewport.spec.ts` asserts
  no `.tile` inside a `.discard-tray` has a box outside that tray's, sampling every
  ~130ms for 90s. Any tile animation near a tray has to happen in an overlay, or on
  a property that doesn't move the box (opacity is fine, transforms are not).
