overlapping the originals — the doubled bevel hidden instead of removed

The other way to get one shared edge out of a run: keep the untouched 3D art and slide each tile over its neighbour's right band. Measured insets from the right edge — 5.5% outline, green to 15.4%, plate and white to 22.5%, face after that. So 22.5% is the overlap that hides the whole band; the tile on top then contributes its own 6.3% left edge as the seam. Nothing above the tiles, so every top band stays visible — which is what a row of real tiles looks like.
