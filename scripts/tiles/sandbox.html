Tile sandbox — the art, lapped and solo

Every tile here is the untouched tiles/*.svg drawn through the app's own classes. In a .tile-lap container each one is drawn 29% wider than its layout box and anchored right, so it bleeds left over the tile before it and hides that tile's right band — the 22.5% of the art that is body and never ink. Sizes are the ones the app uses: 23px hand on a 320px phone, 32px tray, 40px picker, 56px well, plus 96px to see what you are doing.
